Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy describes how the Smile Support Review Tool (the "Application"), operated by Smile Support LLC (合同会社スマイルサポート), Nago City, Okinawa, Japan, handles information.

1. Information we access

After the account owner signs in and grants consent, the Application accesses, through the Google Business Profile API (scope: business.manage), review and business information of Google Business Profiles that Smile Support LLC owns or manages. This may include the review text, star rating, reviewer display name, and the time a review was posted.

2. How we use the information

  • To show new reviews to the owner of the business.
  • To generate draft replies. Review text may be sent to an AI service provider solely for the purpose of generating a draft reply.
  • To post a reply to a review only after the owner has personally reviewed and approved the exact text.

We do not use the information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Sharing

We do not share Google user data with third parties, except (a) the AI service provider described above, acting only on our instructions to generate draft replies, and (b) where required by law. Approved replies are posted to our own Google Business Profiles through Google's API.

4. Storage and retention

Access credentials are stored securely and are used only by the Application. Review data is retained only for as long as needed to manage replies to our own business profiles.

5. Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Revoking access stops the Application from retrieving any further data.
