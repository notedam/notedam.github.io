Smile Support Review Tool

Operator: Smile Support LLC (合同会社スマイルサポート), Nago City, Okinawa, Japan

What this application does

The Smile Support Review Tool is an internal application used only by Smile Support LLC to manage Google Business Profile reviews for the restaurants and bars that we operate ourselves.

With the account owner's consent, the application uses the Google Business Profile API to:

  • Retrieve new reviews posted to our own business profiles.
  • Generate draft replies to those reviews (an AI service may process the review text solely to produce a draft).
  • Post a reply only after the owner has personally reviewed and approved each draft.

Reviews with low ratings or sensitive content are never replied to automatically. The application is not provided to third parties and is never used to access profiles that we do not own or manage.
